Krelzox Remote
Connecting…

Connect to your TV

Type the PIN shown on the TV. It appears in a corner of the screen and in Settings › Krelzox Remote.

Local network only. Nothing leaves your home network.

Waiting for the image…

Tap the image to tap the TV. Drag to swipe, hold for a long press. With a mouse: click, double-click, right-click and wheel.

Move one finger to move the cursor · tap to click · two fingers to scroll

On a PC you can also use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc directly.
